<!-- ========== RESEARCH THRUST 1 ========== -->
<div class="thrust-section">

<h2 style="font-size: 35px;">
        Controlled creation of quantum defects in diamond
  </h2>

  <img
    class="thrust-figure-left"  
    src="{{ '/assets/img/Thrust1_website.png' | relative_url }}"
    alt="Diamond spin systems">
      
  <p>
    We will create group IV spin defects in diamond with controlled coherence, interactions, and spatial localization for applications in quantum communication. By engineering not only the defects but also their local diamond environment, we aim to suppress spectral diffusion and enable opportunities for faster, higher-fidelity entanglement distribution. We will use a variety of synthesis-based tuning knobs to achieve group IV color center creation with precise depth confinement while also improving creation efficiency and charge state stability.
    </p>

  </div>

<div class="thrust-section">

<h2 style="font-size: 35px;"> Monolithic hybrid quantum systems in diamond</h2>

  <img
      class="thrust-figure-left"  
      src="{{ '/assets/img/Thrust2_website.png' | relative_url }}"
      alt="Hybrid quantum systems">

  <p>
      We will create a unified, low-loss materials platform for coherent spin–superconductor coupling and opportunities for novel hybrid quantum systems by achieving spatially-defined superconductivity in diamond through precision boron doping. By spatially defining superconducting and insulating regions through boron doping, our bottom-up approach will replace heterogeneous interfaces with doping-defined electronic transitions. We anticipate that this platform will unlock numerous exciting possibilities for scalable and coherent hybrid quantum technologies.
    </p>
