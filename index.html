Transformative advances in materials science are crucial to realizing the next frontiers in quantum technology. Atomic-scale control will enable new approaches to engineering quantum interactions, protecting coherence, and accessing emerging phases of matter. My group will harness the thermodynamics and kinetics of materials synthesis to control atomic structure, defects, and interfaces, complemented by quantum measurements that probe the local environment and inform materials development. We will pursue this vision by generating spin defects during epitaxial growth, creating controlled surfaces and interfaces through atomic layer processing, and synthesizing new materials for superconducting devices. Together, these efforts will expand the materials toolkit for controlling quantum interactions and coherence. I strive to build an inclusive and collaborative environment that tackles emerging problems at the intersection of materials and quantum science.
