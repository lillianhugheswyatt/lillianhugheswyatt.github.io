A central challenge in realizing robust and scalable quantum technologies is engineering controlled qubit interactions while suppressing unwanted interactions with the surrounding environment. Advances in materials science are crucial to creating quantum platforms with precise control at the atomic scale. My research integrates materials synthesis, characterization, and device fabrication with qubit-based measurements to directly connect materials properties to quantum performance. Our approach will span spin-based and superconducting platforms, developing materials strategies to control noise, decoherence, and interactions. I strive to build an inclusive and collaborative environment that tackles emerging problems at the interface of materials and quantum science.
