I am currently an AWS Quantum Postdoctoral Scholar at Caltech, where I am investigating atomic layer processing of materials for superconducting quantum electronics in the [Minnich group](https://www.minnich.caltech.edu) and collaborating with the [Mirhosseini group](https://qubit.caltech.edu). In Septemeber 2025 I completed my Ph.D. in Materials at the University of California Santa Barbara, under the supervision of [Prof. Ania Bleszynski Jayich](https://www.10-9lab.com) and [Prof. Kunal Mukherjee](https://crystal.stanford.edu) (Stanford), and with the support of an NSF graduate research fellowship. My Ph.D. research focused on creating nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond using plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition growth, and my dissertation, "Engineering interacting spin systems in diamond for quantum technologies," can be found [here](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4dj7h688). During my Ph.D. I also spent 3 months in the group of [Dr. Tokuyuki TERAJI](https://samurai.nims.go.jp/profiles/teraji_tokuyuki?locale=en) at the National Institute for Materials Science (NIMS) in Tsukuba, Japan. Prior to graduate school, I received my B.S. from the University of Richmond where I worked on biosensors functionalized with nanomaterials in the lab of [Prof. Michael Leopold](https://chemistry.richmond.edu/faculty/mleopold/) and was awarded a Goldwater Scholarship. For more details on my academic and professional trajectory, check out my [CV](assets/LillianCV.pdf).

Outside of the lab, you can find me playing music (either bass guitar with my band, [Dead Flowers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apCyiD3FHiI), or violin with the Caltech Orchestra), training for my next marathon, baking, and traveling the world (when time or conferences permit)!
